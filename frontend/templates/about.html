About AI Interview Coach

Empowering software engineers and candidates to excel in high-stakes job interviews.

Our Mission

AI Interview Coach is an intelligent mock interview platform designed to simulate authentic technical, HR, and coding interview scenarios. Powered by advanced AI algorithms, our platform provides actionable feedback on communication clarity, facial expressions, emotion detection, code performance, and resume ATS alignment.

Secure Authentication

Built with Flask-Login, Werkzeug password hashing, and CSRF protection to keep user data private and protected.

AI Performance Analytics

Deep-learning backed scoring models evaluate answers, tone, and technical correctness in real time.

Continuous Improvement

Track historical interview scores across sessions to identify growth areas and build candidate confidence.
